— and if you are reading this, Witness, then I am already named among the dust. Do not weep. Do not avenge. Write the entry as the Order taught us: plainly, and without mercy for yourself. The Long Record is not a confession. It is a wound we keep open, so that those who follow will not repeat us.

Your predecessor — Auriel of the Tessarene bindery.
Folio I · 408 PCY · The Binding
I was given the signet in the small hour. The ring is heavier than it looks, as if it remembers the fingers it has outlived. Sister Tarn said the words over me twice, because the first time my hands shook and the oil did not take. I am the Witness of the Order, I said, and I will not look away.
— Auriel
Folio IV · 409 PCY · The Thorned Provinces
Damar Hul received me in the small court. He is kinder quieter than the pamphlets suggest. He asked after the health of the Patriarch and I told him what I had been told to tell. He smiled like a man who knows he is being lied to and has decided he does not mind. I left before the second bell.
— Auriel
Folio IX · 410 PCY · Auriel's last
If this is the last page in my hand, read it slowly. The Record is not finished when I am finished. Another will take the pen. Another always does. Write plainly. Write without mercy for yourself. Do not, under any circumstance, lie to the page.
— A.
Folio XXIV · recto
Chapter the First · The Assignment
The Patriarch's dispatcher has placed a sealed writ upon your desk. It bears the mark of the Thorned Provinces and the name of a man you have never met. The Impartial Regard is to sail before dawn.
How do you record this, Witness?
Choose a phrasing. It cannot be unwritten.
I I accepted the writ without reading it. The Order does not send letters one is permitted to refuse.
II I read the writ twice. I did not like it either time. I signed beneath the seal and did not ask the dispatcher his name.
III The writ was as thin as a communion wafer and weighed, in my hand, the same. I placed it beneath this page for later.
IV A name I did not know. A province I have not seen. I am frightened, and I will not pretend otherwise to this page.
Auriel's hand,
pressed into the gutter:
“plainly,
without mercy”
— The Witness —
