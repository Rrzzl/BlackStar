# Black Star — Glossary v8.1

Every proper noun and key mechanic in the project with a one-line definition. Quick reference for GPT Image, Claude Code, and new chat sessions. Snapshot date: 2026-04-23 (evening). Aligned to world-bible-v8-1.md. Companion to: world-bible-v8-1.md, black-star-handoff-v8-1.md.

Format: Term — category — one-line definition. Entries marked (deferred) have canonical content yet to be written.

## Cosmology and Powers

Black Star — God — the god of successful rebellions and the cycle of rebellion-and-power itself; older than the first Witness; has run the cycle across eternities through many vessels before the Witness-form existed; worshipped by the Order; true name/form/being deferred; manifests visually in night skies as iteration three approaches.

Cycle, The — Conceptual — the repeating pattern of dynasty → decay → rebellion-with-ritual → Black Star wakening → new dynasty; the Black Star's ongoing nature; institutionalized form begins with the first Witness's original walking; looser forms existed before.

Gift, The — Power category — the Black Star's presence in mortal bloodlines; biological surface with religious source; propagates through host bloodlines; varies in expression.

Visible, The — Gift subtype — Vesh children born with obvious anomaly (extra vertebrae, mirrored organs, skin that responds to light); killed at birth historically.

Hybrid, The — Gift subtype — Vesh descendants bearing traits of other species; Nadezh Vol is sleepless, fifty-one years awake.

Gifted, The — Gift subtype — born normal in appearance, touched in other ways (prescience, perfect memory, persuasion, language fluency); Auriel and Elara are Gifted.

Touched, The — Gift subtype — children with a specific dangerous pattern recurring every three-to-four generations; killed by the Order when found.

Resonant Gifted — Gift subtype — rare variant that can briefly borrow the gift of anyone whose blood they have tasted; Elara is this; Miriath was this; the cycle structurally produces a Resonant Gifted in the host bloodline at each ritual-ripe moment because the ritual requires one.

Priest-host — Role — the Black Star's direct, concentrated possession channel; the Witness, in both his walking and sleeping states.

Throne-host — Role — the Black Star's distributed, inherited possession channel; the current ruling bloodline (Vesh) via the imperial seat.

Gift infrastructure — System — the galaxy-scale logistics, communications, and jump-route stability that Vesh-Gift-possession has passively maintained; failing visibly since the emperor's death.

## History

Ruling Before, The — Dynasty — the dynasty before the current-recorded-history era; ruled for thousands of years; overthrown in iteration one; records destroyed; true name deferred.

First-recorded dynasty — Dynasty — the dynasty between the Ruling Before and the current Vesh dynasty; was itself overthrown by the current Vesh in iteration two; extinguished.

Miriath — Person — last heir of the first-recorded dynasty; killed by the first Witness in iteration two's ritual climax; Resonant Gifted; her death was the bridging sacrifice that completed iteration two's ritual; structurally parallel to Elara in iteration three.

Vesh, House of — Dynasty — the current ruling house; installed as imperial dynasty after iteration two; long rule (more than a thousand years); carries the current throne-host arrangement; destabilizing under iteration-three pressure.

Iteration zero — Historical event (deliberately unknowable) — whether anything preceded the Ruling Before. Game does not answer.

Pre-institutional Black Star work — Historical period (deliberately unknowable) — what the god did through vessels before the first Witness existed; permanently out of reach.

Iteration one — Historical event — the Ruling Before → first-recorded-dynasty transition; the first Witness's first walking; the founding of the current institutional cycle.

Iteration two — Historical event — the first-recorded-dynasty → Vesh transition, roughly a thousand or more years ago; galaxy-wide civil war; the first Witness walked again and killed Miriath; the Order formalized as a public state institution afterward.

Iteration three — Current event — the turning the Fourth faction intends to perform; Elara as sacrifice; conditions ripening since the emperor's death.

Rebellion, The — Historical event — iteration two's civil war; placeholder name pending; the war that installed the Vesh and formalized the Order publicly.

Crown Wars — Historical event — civil conflict in Damar Hul's era; Damar committed genocide; pattern echoes the first Witness's iteration-one military atrocity.

## The First Witness's Original Deeds (v8.1)

Original Deeds, The — Eight specific historical atrocities committed by the first Witness during his original walking; public galactic history; each produced a preserved relic; four are founding-act (political/documentary) and four are channeling-act (direct/cosmic).

Long List, The — Example original deed — the first Witness stood in a city's public square and read aloud the names of two hundred citizens; by sunset all two hundred were dead, killed by their own neighbors, guards, and families; the city surrendered; the scroll of names is an example of a preserved relic.

Founding-act deeds — Category of original deed — political, documentary, social atrocities; the first Witness as institution-builder; relics yield authority-register powers (naming, marking, social unbecoming).

Channeling-act deeds — Category of original deed — direct, physical, cosmic-scale atrocities; the first Witness as god-in-flesh during his most direct channelings; relics yield combat-register powers (fire, devastating force).

Specific eight deed names — Workshop pending; the Long List is the working example.

## The Witness Order

Witness Order, The — Institution — the fanatical cult of the Black Star disguised as a politically-neutral Concord administration institution; internally older than the Vesh dynasty; grew up around the first Witness partly as priesthood, partly as containment apparatus; formalized publicly during iteration two's rebellion.

Witness, The — Role — the Order's apex instrument; there is only one Witness, ever, across all of history; summoned when needed, sleeps otherwise; the player character; the chamber figure is his sleeping self.

Chamber figure, The — Role — the Witness in his sleeping state; sealed beneath Tessarene; same being as the player; bound there partly as vessel, partly as punishment for his original deeds; consent to becoming the vessel has not survived in his own memory — his wound.

Containment function — Order role — tier-4 knowledge; the Order's founding purpose is dual with priesthood; the rites that summon the Witness are also the rites that ensure he returns to the chamber rather than walking free.

Taker / Takers — Class — the Order's identity-erased enforcer class; visually identical; hooded, faceless, silent, stagehand-tier combat threats; full design lock complete.

Keeper / Keepers — Class — the Order's medical class; physicians, surgeons, midwives, poisoners, palliative specialists; perform Touched killings; Imrys is one; full class lock pending.

Inquisitor / Inquisitors — Class — investigators of claimed Witness misconduct during specific wakings; trained in Witness methods; full class lock pending.

Scribe / Scribes — Class — the Order's archivists; maintain Long Record master volumes; Tessarene bindery sisters; full class lock pending.

Elder / Elders — Class — the Order's governance layer; small council (9–15 galaxy-wide); rise from other classes into institutional leadership.

Orthodox, The — Faction — believe the Order's traditional practices are correct; Vassen Kir leads; contains sleeper Fourth members.

Prescient, The — Faction — Sevasti leads; believe the Order must prevent the collapse it sees coming; forging dead-claimant edicts for three years.

Dissolved, The — Faction — heretical; believe the Order itself is the ongoing crime; Olen Rho leads.

Fourth, The — Faction — not acknowledged; the Order's most theologically orthodox sect; want iteration three to turn; engineered the current Witness's waking; intend Elara as the ritual sacrifice.

Reformers — Moral temperament — Order members trying to change the Order from within; old, tired.

Butchers — Moral temperament — Order members who enjoy the cruelty.

Merchants — Moral temperament — Order members selling signet authority and indulging corruption.

Liturgists — Moral temperament — true believers in "the math"; exhausted; Sevasti is one.

Hollow, The (moral temperament) — Order members who have stopped believing anything but cannot leave.

Knowledge Gradient — System — the five tiers of initiation into the Order's deep theology; tier 0 public, tier 4 the Fourth + chamber figure.

Signet, The — Artifact — the Order's three-concentric-rings icon (stylized Black Star); worn by the Witness; his own signet across all wakings; carries memory of prior wakings; the dreaming-whisper artifact.

Rust-red thread — Symbol — stitching at Witness collars and cuffs; the Order's private pain; Takers do not have it.

Long Record, The — Artifact / System — the Witness's personal diary across all his wakings; every entry is his, across many hands and centuries; the save system; writing commits; "cannot be unwritten"; deeper pages unlock through the Witness-Elara-relic loop; carries kill-aftermath entries in the current hand, Elara's own entries in her hand, and memory-return entries in the Witness's hand from centuries ago.

Memory-return entries — Long Record content — entries in the Witness's own hand from centuries ago, describing one of the eight original deeds; appear when a relic is opened by Elara AND the corresponding environmental trigger was activated in the realm; hunter's-path content.

Body-Bell-Binding, The — Ritual — the Order's ritual for the Witness's five-act opening sequence (Acts 0–IV), replayed on every death or summoning.

Tessarene — Place — the planet where the Order's motherhouse is located; contains Korien's vault and, below it, the chamber figure's chamber.

Tessarene bindery sisters — Characters — two elderly Scribes at the motherhouse; maintain Long Record master volumes; names deferred.

Korien's vault — Place — the vault beneath Tessarene containing Korien's research into the cycle; Second Mid-Game Turn location; reveals the Order's institutional founding crimes but NOT the one-Witness cosmology.

Reformed Covenant, The — Institutional outcome — the post-Ending-ζ version of the Order; Touched killings codified as public law; the cult operating openly.

Blood-taking ritual — Mechanic — the first ritual the Witness and Elara perform; early-game story beat; establishes the bridge between Vesh and Witness that makes relic-opening possible; heretical by Order doctrine.

Witness-Elara-relic loop — Progression mechanic — the game's primary leveling system; Witness retrieves relic from claimant realm, brings to Elara, Elara opens it, Witness gains power; relic remains on display in ship cabin.

Relic-opening — Mechanic — Elara's Resonant Gift extracting the method/technique from a relic of an original deed; does not consume the relic; yields a Witness power.

Environmental memory-trigger — Mechanic — a specific event or interaction in a claimant's realm that "wakes" a relic's history; if triggered, subsequent Elara-opening also produces a Long Record memory-return entry; hunter-only content.

## The Nine Claimants

Each claim has both a political claimant and a Vesh heir at its center. The claimant-heir relationship varies per realm (sometimes distinct figures, sometimes the same person, sometimes a regent arrangement). Each realm has Bloodborne-tier beasts. Each realm yields a relic tied to one of the first Witness's eight original deeds. Boss fights yield relics.

Damar Hul — Claimant — The Penitent King, Thorned Provinces; carves prayer-sigils into his skin; Gifted with pain empathy; committed genocide in Crown Wars; holds a disfigured Vesh cousin he protects as penance; realm yields channeling-act relic; original-deed connection: iteration-one military atrocity.

Liana Arvaux — Claimant — Perfected Coast, bio-lab; breeding her line perfectly because her ninth heir is Touched; Vaspar is one of her failed children, released; realm yields founding-act relic; original-deed connection: iteration-one bloodline imposition.

Aldric, the Dreaming Patriarch — Claimant — can be woken, which destroys his grandchildren who have ruled in his stead; Vesh heir is one of those grandchildren, Gift-fragmented from Aldric; realm yields founding-act relic (dream-binding).

The Glass Empress — Claimant — court realm; her ninth heir is six years old, Gifted; treats the child as a political asset; realm yields founding-act relic (ceremonial).

Kroft — Claimant — cybernetic, dangerous; wants a Witness for his laboratory; Vesh heir half-converted to cybernetics against her will; Ending γ refusal variant delivers the player to him to die; realm yields channeling-act relic (raw-power).

Ser Nadezh Vol — Claimant — The Exile, The Verge; Hybrid (sleepless, 51 years awake); runs an orphanage hiding Verge-born Vesh descendants; hides Elara; Nadezh is Vesh herself — is both claim and heir; realm yields channeling-act relic (sleepless-resonance).

The Sevened — Claimant — New Concord, Senate Stars; collective governance; Counselor Ise Varn has reasoned out most of the bloodline truth; their Vesh heir is a senator with perfect memory serving willingly as a living archive; realm yields founding-act relic (constitutional).

Ise Varn — Character — Counselor of the Sevened; Ending γ operator; offers constitutional stability purchased with Elara's specific death.

The Hollow (Name of Serev) — Claimant — Orphan Worlds; Andrei Serev is dead; Sevasti forges edicts under his name; teenage Vesh heir who doesn't know he's Vesh believes he is Serev's legitimate son; realm yields channeling-act relic.

Andrei Serev — Character (deceased) — the dead claimant whose name Sevasti forges.

Ninth claimant (placeholder) — Claimant (deferred) — current candidate concept: a figure who doesn't want the throne and has been trying to decline, being overruled by their own political structure; concept not locked.

## Game Systems

Opening Sequence — System — five-act ritual (0 Body, I Bell, II Binding, III Taking, IV Desk); replayed on every death/summoning.

Act 0 — The Body — Opening Sequence component — dead-Witness composition; two Takers present; one retrieves the signet, one lifts the Long Record.

Act I — The Bell — Opening Sequence component — the bell, the prayer-rite writing itself, the name-scroll, the title card.

Act II — The Binding — Opening Sequence component — Witness Creation; appearance, background (cover story), disposition, name; hot-wax signature commit.

Act III — The Taking — Opening Sequence component — 20–30 second illustrated scene of the Takers taking the falling Witness; theatrical cover-story performance for public consumption.

Act IV — The Desk — Opening Sequence component — waking at the desk; Sevasti's letter open; first mission sends the Witness to retrieve Elara from Nadezh's orphanage.

Six backgrounds — Character creation — Former Soldier, Disgraced Theologian, Physician of the Verge, Lapsed Aristocrat, Born in the Orphan Worlds, No Record; all six are cover stories prepared for this waking; "No Record" is closest to truth; each has a specific cracking moment authored (pending).

The Taking — Ritual/Scene — Act III public performance; also the name of the enforcer class's work.

Nine endings — System — α Name Elara, β Name Nadezh, γ Dissolve with the Sevened, δ Burn the Record, ε Take the Throne, ζ The Sacrifice (hidden), η Kill the Chamber Figure (hidden), θ Release the Chamber Figure (hidden, apocalyptic), ι Return to the Chamber (hidden, voluntary dormancy).

Ending-specific kill requirements — System — each surface ending requires a specific subset of claimants killed and/or spared; not shown as UI checklist; emerges through political logic and dialogue; some endings become unavailable mid-game based on player choices.

First Mid-Game Turn — The Dossier — Story beat — ~30% through; Witness discovers Adhvi's unfinished confession, which is his own from the prior waking.

Second Mid-Game Turn — The Vault — Story beat — ~60% through; Witness reads Korien's research in Tessarene vault; reveals Order's founding crimes but NOT the one-Witness cosmology.

Third Mid-Game Turn — The Companion — Story beat — ~70-85% through; closest companion revealed as deliberately placed.

Approach to the Chamber — Story beat — late game only, deep-lore path only; the Witness descends beneath Tessarene; the reveal of his one-being nature.

Open-world structure — Game design — after the fixed opening (retrieve Elara from Nadezh's Verge), the galaxy opens; seven-plus realms available in any order; difficulty communicated diegetically through enemy behavior.

Diegetic difficulty — Design principle — realms have intrinsic difficulty tiers but the game does not label them; enemy behavior teaches the player whether they are ready for a realm.

Aftermath layer — System — consequences of player choices in each realm are reported back to the Witness through bridge console news, letters at the cabin desk, NPC dialogue on later realms, and Long Record entries; three or four claimants get full authored aftermath, the rest carry generic acknowledgment; triggers tied to game-clock (realms completed, rest periods) not sequence position.

GRRM-route — Design principle (Rule 13) — kills do not feel morally proportional; sparing has its own costs; moral arithmetic never balances.

Iconic but Variant — Design principle — every Witness immediately recognizable as Order, no two identical across wakings (though the player now learns all variants were the same being).

Iconic Identical — Design principle — all Takers visually identical; one canonical image serves every Taker; uniformity is the design thesis.

Three Beats (Taker individuality reveal) — Design pattern — Takers revealed as people across three escalating game moments: the fallen hood, the protocol break, the former-Taker NPC.

Elara-Witness-Taker triangle — Design framework — Elara is the cost, the Witness is the choice, the Taker is the mechanism.

Signature colors — Design principle — each major character gets at most one small non-palette detail tied to interiority; Elara starlight blue, Witness nothing, Taker nothing.

Surface game / Deep game — Design principle — Hard Rule 9; the surface game is the nine-claimant succession, the deep game is the Witness's identity and the cycle.

Hidden ritual ending — Design principle (Rule 12) — the ritual ending is genuinely hidden; many players never find it across many playthroughs; the game does not nudge toward it.

Revelation drip-feed — Mechanic — the player learns they are the sole Witness across history through five channels: NPC recognition, environmental storytelling, Long Record memory-return entries, Ioran Kest, and the chamber meeting; all five genuinely hidden per Rule 12.

Hunter's path — Informal term — the collection of in-game content that connects the first Witness's deeds to the current Witness; requires environmental memory-triggers in realms, Ioran Kest discovery, reading Long Record deeper entries, finding NPC-recognition moments; genuinely hidden.

Founding-act powers — Power register — authority, naming, documentary cruelty, social unbecoming; from relics tied to the first Witness's founding atrocities; roughly four of the eight powers.

Channeling-act powers — Power register — direct combat force; from relics tied to the first Witness's god-in-flesh deeds; roughly four of the eight powers; supports conventional combat scaling.
